# Privacy Policy

Scripture does not collect or share personal data. It works offline and
does not use accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

The app saves your chosen edition, recent reading positions, and highlights on
your device so you can pick up where you left off. You can clear highlights in
the reader. To remove all local app data, delete the app from your device.

Website links open in your browser. The email link opens your mail app. If you
email me for support, I use your email address and message to respond and handle
your request. I keep support emails as needed to handle the request and refer
to it if you follow up. You can ask me to delete your support email at any time.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email
[phil@ptx.sh](mailto:phil@ptx.sh).
