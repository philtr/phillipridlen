# Bible for iPhone and iPad

Read Scripture offline, switch between 15 editions, and pick up where you left
off. Bible brings together modern and historic English translations and two
translation aids.

- Jump to any book and chapter in your current edition.
- Tap a footnote number to read the note, then jump back to your place.
- Highlight a single word, a phrase, or a whole verse. Copy verses to share.

Bible is currently in testing.
